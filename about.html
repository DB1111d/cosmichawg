Live Music✦Funky Roots

Cosmic Hawg was born in Greenville, South Carolina in 2025 — the product of a years-long weekend jam in Jeff's garage and a shared obsession with music that refuses to sit still long enough to be categorized.

We pull from the full spectrum of American sound: funk, soul, rock, and blues — the kind of sprawling, groove-heavy music that moves a room without asking permission. Part funky, part cosmic drift.

The name says it all. Something earthy and real, something that drifts a little too far into the stars. We play to that tension every time we step on a stage.
